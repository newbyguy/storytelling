How to use your week
1

Pick & shape

Take the weekly story (or search your own topic), choose the theme — safety, efficiency, quality — and who you're talking to. The builder assembles a ready-to-speak script.

2

Practice out loud

Any weeknight, open Practice mode: teleprompter, timer, cue cards, and a recorder so you can hear yourself. Two run-throughs beat ten silent read-throughs.

3

Tell it Wednesday

Open the meeting with the story, land the bridge line, then roll into your numbers — financials, efficiency, what's new, upcoming training. Mark it "told" so you never repeat one.
